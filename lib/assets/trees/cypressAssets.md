# Mediterranean cypress textures

Original images generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 26 October 2026.
Selected outputs were resized to 512 × 512 with `sips`; alpha is preserved.
No botanical photograph or private reference pixels enter these assets.

- `cypressSpray.png`: a tightly ascending spray of scale-covered branchlets.
  The original had individually bright scales. Its colour-only revision
  keeps the fine cutout silhouette and makes the interior a quiet grey-green.
- `cypressBark.png`: lightly furrowed grey-brown fibrous bark, with narrow
  interrupted vertical ridges and restrained warm/cool variation. Albedo only.

Prompt record:

1. Upright dense Mediterranean cypress spray, not spruce; tiny rounded
   scale-covered shoots, 6–9 ascending tips, connected irregular tapered
   silhouette and fine evergreen edge. Muted sage grey-green, quiet interior,
   flat diffuse lighting, no broad leaves, cones, brown stem, text or shadows.
   Actual transparent background.
2. Preserve alpha, holes, silhouette and edge detail. Remove pale outlined
   branchlets and individual scale highlights. Quiet mid grey-green near
   sRGB (93,120,98), subtle broad patches, no dark twig lines.
3. Seamless grey-brown cypress bark, long interrupted vertical fibrous ridges,
   narrow irregular flakes, subtle warm shallow fissures and cool grey faces.
   Restrained contrast, flat lighting, no deep black cracks, moss, knots,
   perspective, shadows or text.
