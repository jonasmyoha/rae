Physics scaling

The physics playground's box pyramid (Box3D's large pyramid, Z-up) on both physics tracks: track A, the Rae port of Box3D (examples/122_physics_playground_port), and track B, Box3D's own C library through Rae's ECS (examples/123_physics_playground_c). Each case builds the pyramid, turns sleeping off, settles 20 fixed 60 Hz steps (4 sub-steps) and reports the median of the next 100. Regenerate with benchmarks/physics_scaling/run.sh; the analysis is in docs/physics-performance-plan.md §10e.
